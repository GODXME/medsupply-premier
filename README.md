# MedSupply Premier

# PREMIUM MEDICAL SUPPLIES COMPANY — COMPLETE WEBSITE DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT PROMPT

## 1. Project Overview

Design and develop a world-class, premium, modern and fully responsive corporate website for a medical healthcare products manufacturing and distribution company.

The company specializes in high-quality medical consumables, healthcare textiles, wound-care products, protective medical equipment and patient-care products.

The website must communicate:

- Medical-grade quality
- Product safety and reliability
- Professional manufacturing standards
- Hygiene and protection
- Institutional trust
- Global supply capabilities

**Primary target customers:**
Hospitals, nursing homes, clinics, pharmacies, healthcare institutions, medical distributors, wholesalers and procurement departments.

The overall design should look like an established international medical supplies brand, not a generic medical ecommerce template.

## 2. Brand Identity & Colour System

IMPORTANT: Use the exact colour palette, visual identity and design language from the design reference image provided by the client.

Before implementation:
- Analyze the reference design.
- Extract its primary, secondary and accent colours.
- Identify background colours, gradients, typography and contrast.
- Reproduce the same visual language consistently throughout the website.
- Do not introduce unrelated colours.
- Maintain premium readability and visual hierarchy.

Create a centralized design-token system so all colours can be updated easily.

## 3. Hero Section — Premium 3D Experience

Create a visually impressive, cinematic hero section.

**Headline:**
"Advanced Medical Essentials. Trusted Healthcare Protection."

**Supporting text:**
"Delivering dependable medical consumables and patient-care solutions through quality, precision and commitment to better healthcare."

**Primary CTA:** Explore Our Products

**Secondary CTA:** Request a Business Enquiry

### Hero visual direction

Create a premium 3D medical product composition featuring:
- Rolled cotton wool.
- Cotton crepe bandage.
- Elastic compression bandage.
- Zinc oxide adhesive plaster roll.
- Adult incontinence pants.
- Disposable face mask.
- Bouffant head cover.
- Examination gloves.

Display the products in an elegant floating arrangement with realistic materials, soft shadows, controlled reflections and dimensional depth.

The scene should feel like a luxury medical product advertisement.

Avoid:
- Cartoonish 3D objects.
- Excessive neon effects.
- Overloaded animations.
- Unnecessary medical symbols.
- Generic hospital stock imagery.

## 4. OriginKit Animation & 3D Integration

Reference website: https://www.originkit.dev/

Explore OriginKit's animated components, WebGL effects, 3D visuals and interactive hero sections.

Use suitable components as inspiration or integrate their available source code where appropriate.

Recommended implementation:

1. **3D Hero Scene:** Floating medical products with realistic lighting, subtle rotation and depth.
2. **Particle Background:** Minimal, elegant particles that create a sophisticated technological atmosphere.
3. **Text Reveal:** Smooth headline entrance animation.
4. **Scroll Reveal:** Product sections animate into view naturally.
5. **Interactive Product Cards:** Gentle 3D tilt and elevation on hover.
6. **Button Interaction:** Premium animated buttons with subtle transitions.
7. **Ambient Background:** Soft gradient lighting and subtle motion.

Keep animations lightweight and optimized.

Use Three.js or React Three Fiber only where necessary. Use CSS transforms and lightweight animations for simpler effects.

Respect reduced-motion settings and provide a static fallback on mobile devices.

## 15. Final Design Quality

The final website should look like a premium international medical supplies manufacturer and distributor.

The 3D hero should immediately communicate the company's complete product portfolio.

The product catalogue should be the main focus of the experience.

The website must balance visual creativity with business credibility.

Do not make the website look like:
- A generic hospital website.
- A pharmacy ecommerce template.
- A basic Bootstrap landing page.
- A gaming-style neon website.
- An overanimated experimental portfolio.
